Movement review

Each scene first plays with every control on, then each control on its own, with every other control of that scene held still. The caption in the clip shows the chain: the control, the transformations feeding it, and the music signals feeding those. The music is the built-in 120 BPM test track, its slow changes 3× faster to fit a short clip.

Cards come from the app itself (clips.js, written by tools/record_solos.sh), so they follow its controls. Mark each one and add notes, then press Copy all notes and paste them into the chat. Notes stay in this browser; a card says when its clip was re-recorded after your note.
